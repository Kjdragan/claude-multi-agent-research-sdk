## Key Military Developments

### 1. Failed Easter Truce (April 20, 2025)

**Russian Initiative:**
- President Vladimir Putin announced a 30-hour Easter ceasefire from Saturday 16:00 BST to Sunday 22:00 BST
- Kremlin stated no orders were given to extend the truce beyond midnight

**Ukrainian Response:**
- President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called Putin's words "empty"
- Ukrainian military reported over 46 Russian assaults since the start of Easter Sunday
- More than 2,000 alleged ceasefire violations by Russian forces

**Mutual Accusations:**
- Russia claimed Ukraine launched drone attacks "hundreds of times"
- Ukraine reported continuous Russian shelling and drone activity
- Both sides traded blame for violating the temporary truce

### 2. Advanced Drone Warfare Innovations

**Ukrainian Technological Breakthrough:**
- **Magura V7 System**: 24-foot remote-controlled speedboat launching antiaircraft missiles
- **Combat Success**: First-ever use of drone boat to shoot down fighter aircraft
- **Confirmed Kills**: Two Russian Su-30 warplanes downed in May 2025
- Presented to media by military intelligence chief Gen. Kyrylo Budanov on May 14

**Strategic Drone Operations:**
- Ukrainian SBU drones struck Primorsk, Russia's largest Baltic Sea oil-loading port
- Attack caused fires at vessels and pumping stations, suspending oil shipments
- Targeting of Russia's "shadow fleet" used to evade international sanctions
- Long-distance drone strikes on Russian military airfields and gas pipelines

### 3. Infrastructure and Economic Warfare

**Russian Oil Infrastructure Targeted:**
- **Primorsk Port Strike**: Key hub for 60 million tons of oil annually (~$15 billion revenue)
- **Strategic Impact**: Disruption of Russia's sanction-evasion shipping network
- **Military Success**: Fires broke out at vessels and pumping stations

**Ongoing Military Operations:**
- **Kyiv Attacks**: Russia launched missile attacks, including worst airstrike of 2025
- **Casualties**: At least 31 killed (including 5 children) and 150+ wounded
- **Russian Strikes**: Continued missile and drone attacks on Ukrainian cities
- **Ukrainian Counterstrikes**: Three people killed, two wounded in Russian territory

### 4. Peace Negotiations and Diplomatic Activity

**Istanbul Peace Talks (July 23, 2025):**
- Third round of talks between Ukrainian and Russian delegations
- Meeting lasted less than an hour
- Ukraine proposed Zelensky-Putin summit before end of August
- Focus on prisoner exchanges and humanitarian issues

**Black Sea Ceasefire Agreement:**
- White House-brokered agreement for safe commercial shipping
- Halt to military strikes over Black Sea
- Russia demanded sanctions relief on banks, insurers, food exporters
- Zelensky accused Russia of "lying again" about terms

**Prisoner Exchange Developments:**
- Both sides agreed to massive prisoner swap
- Repatriation of bodies of thousands of soldiers
- Ukraine presented list of children allegedly abducted and taken to Russia
- Russia presented peace memorandum calling for Ukrainian territory cession

### 5. International Military Response

**NATO and Allied Support:**
- **UK Military**: Two fighter jets intercepted Russian aircraft over Baltic Sea
- **Poland-Ukraine Cooperation**: Drone interception training on Polish territory
- **UK Financial Support**: $193 million in aid plus new sanctions against Russia
- **International Visits**: Prince Harry, Polish and UK foreign ministers visited Kyiv

**Belarus Involvement:**
- Threats to shift Zapad-2025 military drills closer to NATO border
- Increased regional tension through military posturing
