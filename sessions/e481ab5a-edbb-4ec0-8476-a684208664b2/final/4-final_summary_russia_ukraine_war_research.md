## 1. Session Overview and Key Findings

### Research Scope and Objectives
This research session comprehensively analyzed the latest military activities in the Russia-Ukraine War, focusing on developments from April through July 2025. The investigation successfully captured both military operations and diplomatic initiatives, providing a complete picture of the conflict's current state.

### Principal Findings

**Critical Military Developments:**
- **Failed Easter Ceasefire (April 20, 2025):** Russia's 30-hour truce proposal collapsed with Ukraine reporting 2,000+ violations and 46 Russian assaults during the period
- **Revolutionary Drone Warfare:** Ukraine's Magura V7 system achieved first-ever combat kills of fighter aircraft by drone boats, downing two Russian Su-30 warplanes
- **Strategic Infrastructure Targeting:** Ukrainian drone strikes on Primorsk port disrupted Russia's $15 billion annual oil revenue through sanction-evasion shipping
- **Escalating Urban Warfare:** Russia conducted what Ukraine called the "worst airstrike of 2025" on Kyiv, killing 31 civilians including 5 children

**Diplomatic Parallel Activity:**
- Third round of Istanbul peace talks (July 23, 2025) lasted less than one hour
- White House-brokered Black Sea ceasefire agreement with ongoing disputes over terms
- Both sides agreed to massive prisoner exchanges and soldier body repatriation

**International Military Response:**
- NATO allies maintained active support through air patrols and financial assistance
- UK provided $193 million in aid plus enhanced sanctions against Russia
- Belarus threatened regional escalation through military drill repositioning

### Statistical Impact Assessment
- **Russian Casualties:** Estimated 80,000 during Kursk incursion (Ukrainian assessment)
- **Economic Disruption:** $15 billion in annual oil revenue impacted through Primorsk port attack
- **Ceasefire Violations:** 2,000+ documented Russian violations during Easter truce
- **Civilian Impact:** 31 killed, 150+ wounded in single Kyiv airstrike
